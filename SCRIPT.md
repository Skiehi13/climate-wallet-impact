# Presentation Script (7.5 minutes)

## HERO [0:00-0:40]
Most climate presentations talk about coffee or flights getting expensive. Let's discuss what nobody wants to bring up: Therapy bills. Gas masks. Funeral costs. Let's talk about what climate change is actually going to cost you.

[SCROLL to Mental Health]

## MENTAL HEALTH [0:40-2:25]
Climate anxiety isn't just a buzzword. It's a diagnosable condition driving therapy demand through the roof.

[POINT to stats] 75% of young people say the future is frightening. 45% report it affects their daily functioning.

Most insurance doesn't cover climate-specific anxiety. You're paying out-of-pocket with 6-12 week waitlists.

[USE calculator]
- 2 sessions/month at $150 = $3,600/year
- 25% increase over 5 years
[CLICK Calculate]
- Projected: $4,500 annual
- Additional $4,500 over 5 years

[NOTE top-right counter updating]

## EMERGENCY SUPPLIES [2:25-4:30]
Emergency supplies used to be occasional purchases. Not anymore.

In California—Sacramento, Roseville—wildfire season means 2-4 months of hazardous air annually.

N95 masks, air purifiers, go-bags aren't prepper gear. They're normal household expenses.

[CHECK boxes slowly]
- N95 masks: $200
- Air filters: $150  
- Food/water: $300
- First aid: $100
- Batteries: $150
- Go-bag: $200

Annual restocking: $1,100
10-year projection: $11,000

FEMA says 72 hours. CalOES says 2 weeks. Do the math.

## FUNERAL SERVICES [4:30-6:15]
Let's talk about death. Climate change is killing people right now.

2021 heat dome: 1,400 deaths
Europe 2022: 61,000 excess deaths
WHO projection: 250,000 additional deaths annually by 2030-2050

Who's dying? Elderly, unhoused, outdoor workers. The people who can't afford AC or evacuation.

[USE calculator]
- Traditional burial + plot: ~$10,000
- Climate increase 15%
- Disaster scenario
[CLICK Calculate]
- Final cost: $12,000+
- Additional: $2,000+

These aren't statistics. They're funerals someone paid for.

## CONCLUSION [6:15-7:30]
[SHOW table]

Mental health: $3,600-$14,400 annually, hits young people
Emergency: $500-$1,100 annually, climate-vulnerable regions  
Funeral: $8,000-$15,000, elderly/unhoused/workers

These are costs for MANAGING climate impacts, not preventing them. We're budgeting for trauma response, survival gear, and death.

Mental health helps us cope with watching the world burn. Emergency supplies help us survive the burning. Funerals are what happens when those aren't enough.

**That's your climate tax. And it's only going up.**

The real question: are we budgeting for adaptation, or should we be investing in actually fixing this?

## TIPS
- Don't rush statistics
- Pause after death tolls
- Let calculators display fully
- Interact naturally
- Your dry humor works in opening/mental health
- Stay serious for funeral section
- End reflective, not preachy
